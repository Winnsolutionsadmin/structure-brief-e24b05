Three paths we can pursue.
You pick the shape. We build it together.

Jarred Winn × Carter Razink. These are not terms being handed down - they are the three structures I am genuinely open to, each fully worked. They share the same foundations and differ in one honest trade: where your upside concentrates - NewCo itself, or the equity we take in the companies we transform - and when you want to commit. Pick one, mix them, or tell me what is missing.

Shared foundations (identical in every path)

  • Cash covers costs and opportunity cost - it is not the early goal. The goal is equity: in NewCo and in the companies we transform. Both of us bill our time at fair cost in every path; neither of us is here to build a cash cow, and early profits reinvest by default.
  • Mutual exclusivity, both directions: I pursue no engineering-improvement offerings without you; you pursue no agentic-transformation work without me. Plain-English scope: named markets and clients, clean termination, carve-outs for your background projects and your engineers' own IP.
  • Your background framework stays yours - licensed in, never taken. Go-forward work is built clean inside NewCo, exactly as you proposed. Engineers join by contingent offer.
  • The 90-day pilot criteria are written together, up front - what success means, what it unlocks, and what happens if we miss. No hidden rules in any path.
  • Origination pays the pioneer, for both of us: on a client's collected gross profit, whoever brought them earns: clients #1-5: 30% yrs 1-2, then 20%, then 10% · #6-10: 25/15/10 · #11+: 15/10/5. 60/40 when we both touch a deal. Your CSE introduction is a founding-cohort client.
  • Delivery profit splits by per-project allocations agreed at kickoff. No timesheets.
  • Team pool: 5% reserved for the engineers we bring in - your nominations.
Path A

The Earn-In Ladder

For: maximum ownership; you want to be a co-founder in title AND economics, earned on delivery.
  • Co-Founder, Engineering - day one, with a 4% signing grant (profits interests, 4yr/1yr).
  • Milestone tranches to a 17% part-time ceiling: pilot delivered +3, stack v1 live +3, measured client productivity lift +3, commercial gates +2/+2.
  • Full-time kicker +8% to a 25% cap, with 100% acceleration on any change of control - earned equity is paid equity at an exit.
  • Illustration at a $30M outcome: $5.1M part-time laddered, $7.5M full-time.

Full detail: the Path A one-pager · the branch map.

Path B

The Portfolio Path

For: you believe most in the equity we capture in the companies we transform - upside weighted to the client portfolio over the NewCo cap table.
  • Same title track, compact NewCo ladder to a 10% cap (2% signing, smaller tranches).
  • In exchange: a guaranteed 40% share of every client-equity stake captured on engagements you lead - versus the standard engine's variable split. The pilot-for-equity deals ARE the product of this company; this path gives you the biggest direct piece of them.
  • Cash identical to every path: fair-cost rates + your origination earnings. No cash premium, no cash penalty.
  • Honest trade stated plainly: client stakes are less liquid and less certain than NewCo equity, but they are the asset this company exists to accumulate. Going full-time later reopens the Path A ladder by mutual agreement.
Path C

Prove-Then-Choose

For: lowest commitment now; we let 90 days of real data pick the structure.
  • The Anchorage pilot runs under a simple paid collaboration agreement at agreed rates, with exclusivity scoped only to named opportunities (Anchorage + your CSE lead).
  • At day 90 we choose Path A or Path B with the pilot's actual numbers in hand - and the ladder credits the pilot retroactively (you do not lose the tranche by choosing late).
  • No equity, no long exclusivity, no cap-table entry until we both opt in.
  • Cleanest walk-away for both sides if the collaboration is not what we hope.
