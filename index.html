Why a ladder and not a number: a static split has to be wrong in one direction - it either overpays part-time or underpays a great full-time outcome, and the renegotiation is where partnerships die. Here the equity moves only when the work does, with baselines and measurement windows named in writing at grant, partial credit at 70-90% of target, and a neutral third party named for any milestone dispute. Nobody ever has to argue about who caused what.

The cash engine (quarterly, before equity ever matters)

  • Origination - the founding clients pay the most, for both of us. Whoever brings the client earns, on that client's collected gross profit: clients #1-5: 30% in years 1-2, then 20%, then 10% · clients #6-10: 25% / 15% / 10% · clients #11+: 15% / 10% / 5%. Split 60/40 when we both touch a deal. Your CSE introduction would be a founding-cohort client - this premium cuts both ways by design.
  • Delivery. Remaining profit splits by per-project allocations we agree at each project kickoff. No timesheets, no scorekeeping - the allocation is set before the work starts.
  • Client equity. Pilot-for-equity upside flows through the same two rules. Everything is one fairness engine, applied identically to both of us.

Mutual exclusivity and IP

  • Exclusivity is mutual and signs with this package: I pursue no engineering-improvement offerings without you; you pursue no agentic-transformation work without me. Scoped in plain English - named markets and clients, clean termination rights, carve-outs for your background projects and your engineers' own IP.
  • Your background framework stays yours. Licensed in, never taken. Go-forward work is built clean inside NewCo, exactly as you proposed. Your engineers join by contingent offer: their own work stays theirs, what they build here is NewCo's.
  • Leaver terms protect the earner: everything vested is yours to keep on a good-faith exit. Unvested tranches simply lapse. No clawback games.
Fair contribution, fair equity, aligned goals. The vision and the accounts I bring are already built; the engineering pillar is yours to build. This structure pays most, fastest, to whoever moves the company most - starting with the 90 days in front of us.
